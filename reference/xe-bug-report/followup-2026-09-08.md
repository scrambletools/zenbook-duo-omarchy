Follow-up comment for https://gitlab.freedesktop.org/drm/xe/kernel/-/issues/9196, 2026-09-08.

Follow-up after another day of testing, which changes the picture somewhat.

**The failure is not tied to docking at all.** A boot with the detachable keyboard never on the laptop (disk password typed on an external USB keyboard, keyboard docked only after login) still ended with PHY B unusable. The first sign is now visible at boot time: about two seconds after the main system starts (Plymouth re-attaching to DRM), `AUX B/DDI B/PHY B: Failed to write aux backlight level: -110`, then `vblank wait timed out on crtc 1`, `[CRTC:270:pipe B] flip_done timed out`, `pipe_off wait timed out`, `Failed to bring PHY B to idle`, `PHY B failed to change powerdown state`. Any later enable of eDP-2 then fails with the refclk / link training sequence from the original report.

**The bad state persists across a warm reboot and across a quick power-off.** Every boot that followed a wedged boot within a minute or two showed the AUX timeout at Plymouth start, with the firmware still lighting the panel normally at the unlock prompt. What clears it is a full power reset: shutdown, charger unplugged, power button held ~15 s, machine left off for a minute. After that the same kernel, parameters and sequence give a clean boot, and disable/enable cycles of eDP-2 work again. Loading firmware setup defaults also clears it, which is what first made this look like a firmware problem. So whatever is stuck survives S5 with the charger connected but not a real power-down: EC-side panel power sequencing or the panel itself seem more likely than SoC state.

**Ruled out since the report:** `xe.enable_dc=0`, `xe.enable_panel_replay=0` (that one fixes a separate boot-time hang with a burst of "Timed out waiting for PSR Idle for re-enable" on pipe A, and is needed, but has no effect on this), `video=eDP-2:d` to keep the kernel off the connector during boot (the AUX timeout still appears at Plymouth start), AC vs battery, and docking or undocking during boot.

**Unchanged:** a boot with the keyboard docked at power-on (firmware does not light the covered panel) always fails on the first enable of eDP-2, even when the previous boot was healthy. A boot with the keyboard off at power-on after a healthy previous boot always works.

I can attach a full `drm.debug=0x1e` log of a wedged boot from Plymouth start onward, or of the failing enable, if that helps.
